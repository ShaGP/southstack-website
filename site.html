01Our Mission

We exist to open doors for South Asian storytellers.

South Stack Studios is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit on a mission to champion authentic South Asian stories in film and media — and to build real pathways for the people who tell them.

We serve emerging filmmakers, writers, and artists across the global South Asian diaspora, providing the funding, mentorship, and access they need to bring nuanced, original narratives to audiences everywhere. As a nonprofit, every gift and grant goes toward that public benefit: a richer, more representative culture for all.

About · 501(c)(3) Nonprofit

A new home for South Asian stories.

South Stack Studios is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to funding, mentoring, and amplifying emerging South Asian filmmakers, writers, and artists.

Authentic stories, told with the grace they deserve.

South Stack Studios is a nonprofit built to open doors for South Asian storytellers. We believe representation is a public good — that when overlooked communities see themselves on screen, culture becomes richer for everyone.

We invest in original storytellers — filmmakers, writers, and artists — whose work celebrates the richness and complexity of South Asian experiences. Through funding, mentorship, and community partnerships, we empower creators to bring fresh, nuanced narratives to global audiences, amplifying original voices that the market has too often left behind.

Who we serve: emerging South Asian storytellers across the global diaspora — with a focus on those who lack access to traditional financing, mentorship, and distribution.

Our nonprofit status

A registered 501(c)(3) public charity.

South Stack Studios operates under our legal name, Haldi Hub, as a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations are tax-deductible to the fullest extent allowed by law and are used exclusively to advance our charitable mission and programs.

Our Programs

How we invest in South Asian storytellers.

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, our programs are designed to remove the real barriers emerging creators face — money, mentorship, access, and community. Here's how each one works and who it serves.

Program 01

Funding & Producing

Serves: emerging filmmakers & writers
without access to traditional financing

We provide grants and hands-on production support to get high-impact South Asian projects made — stepping in as funder and, where needed, as producer or executive producer. Recent projects we've backed have reached Sundance and earned Oscar qualification.

  • Project grants and production financing
  • Executive-producing and production guidance
  • Support from development through delivery
Program 02

Mentorship & Networking

Serves: creators building craft
and industry relationships

We connect storytellers with award-winning filmmakers, producers, and technologists through masterclasses, workshops, and one-on-one guidance — including our "From Pitch to Premiere" masterclass and main-stage panels at leading festivals and conferences.

  • Masterclasses and skills workshops
  • One-on-one mentorship with industry leaders
  • Introductions across our network of advisors
Program 03

Distribution & Promotion

Serves: finished work seeking
audiences and buyers

We open festival pathways, market access, and marketing support so that our storytellers' work is seen — championing projects at Sundance, Tribeca, Cannes, IFFLA, and the Tasveer Film Market, and connecting creators with global executives.

  • Festival strategy and submissions support
  • Introductions to buyers and distributors
  • Marketing and publicity amplification
Program 04

Community & Culture

Serves: the wider South Asian
creative community

We host galas, mixers, and workshops that connect, celebrate, and recharge emerging talent — building a durable community where South Asian storytellers can find collaborators, mentors, and momentum.

  • Community galas and networking mixers
  • Skill-building workshops and salons
  • Presence at markets like Tasveer connecting stories with executives

Who benefits from our work.

Our beneficiaries are emerging South Asian filmmakers, writers, and artists across the global diaspora — creators with vital stories but limited access to funding, mentorship, and distribution.

Through our support, their work has premiered at Sundance, screened at Tribeca, earned Oscar qualification, and reached global executives at markets and festivals — outcomes that ripple outward into a more representative, more vibrant culture for the 2-billion-strong diaspora and beyond.
